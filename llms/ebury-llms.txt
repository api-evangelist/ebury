# Ebury

> Ebury is a global fintech platform specializing in international payments, foreign exchange (FX), and working capital solutions for businesses. Majority-owned by Banco Santander, Ebury enables companies to pay, collect, hedge, and finance across borders through a single platform.

## Company Overview

Ebury provides global accounts built for ambitious businesses, helping them grow internationally through a unified platform. The company operates across 30+ regulated markets with 50+ offices and 2,000+ multilingual experts, serving growing businesses, enterprises, and institutions.

- **Headquarters:** London, United Kingdom
- **Parent Company:** Majority-owned by Banco Santander
- **Reach:** 160+ countries, 140+ currencies supported
- **Offices:** 50+ locations globally
- **Regulated Markets:** 30+

## Products

### Business Accounts
- Send, manage, and receive global money
- Multi-currency accounts in 29+ currencies across 20+ countries
- Local collection accounts in 30+ countries
- Same-day cross-border transfers
- Mass payments and batch processing
- Corporate cards (virtual and physical) with multi-currency support
- Real-time subsidiary transfers with zero charges
- Custom approval workflows

### Hedge (FX Risk Management)
- Spot FX and limit orders
- Forward contracts (1 week to 10 years)
- Options contracts
- Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs)
- Hedging policy automation with performance tracking
- Unified dashboard for monitoring currency exposure
- Coverage across 60+ currencies (APAC, Europe, LATAM, MENA, North America, Sub-Saharan Africa)

### Financing
- Supplier Payment Finance
- Credit lines from £50,000 to £5 million
- Repayment terms up to 150 days
- No setup costs, no hidden fees, no early repayment charges
- Unsecured (no security required)
- Eligibility: £1M+ revenue, £100K+ tangible net worth, 2+ years trading history
- Integration with hedging solutions for FX risk during repayment

### Connect (APIs & Integrations)
- Business APIs for payments, cash management, hedging, and reconciliation
- Software integrations with 30+ tools (NetSuite, Xero, SAP, Sage, Oracle, Odoo)
- Embedded finance capabilities (white-label, affiliate solutions)
- Webhooks for real-time event notifications
- OAuth2 authentication, DDoS protection
- 99.9% uptime, 24/7 availability
- Backward-compatible APIs
